Flagship solution

Utility Record — digitizing electricity and water meter reporting.

Customers can already pay electricity and water bills digitally through banks and mobile money — but meter reading and reporting often still requires a physical process. Utility Record closes that gap, letting customers register accounts, capture and submit meter readings, and keep a digital history — while providers receive structured, monitorable data.

  • Register electricity or water accounts
  • Capture & submit meter readings
  • Maintain a digital meter history
  • Manage multiple utility accounts
  • Status updates & notifications
  • Fewer unnecessary center visits
