Flagship solution

Utility Record — digitizing electricity and water meter reporting.

Bills are already paid digitally. Meter reporting isn't — yet. Utility Record closes that gap: customers submit readings from their phone, providers get structured data.

  • Register electricity or water accounts
  • Capture & submit meter readings
  • Maintain a digital meter history
  • Manage multiple utility accounts
  • Status updates & notifications
  • Fewer unnecessary center visits
