About Ejersa

Technology. Intelligence. Solutions.

Ejersa Tech & Consult PLC is a technology and business consulting company focused on building practical digital solutions and providing research-driven strategic services. Our work sits at the intersection of technology, business, data, and market intelligence.

Our Vision

To become a leading African technology and intelligence company that transforms everyday problems into scalable digital solutions and better business decisions.

Our Mission

To develop practical technology platforms, generate actionable intelligence and provide strategic consulting that improves how people access services, how businesses compete and how organizations make decisions.

Our Values

The principles that guide our work

Problem First

Understand the problem before designing the solution.

Possibility

We believe difficult problems can be transformed into practical opportunities.

Evidence

Good decisions should be supported by data and research.

Innovation

We continuously search for better ways to solve problems.

Impact

Integrity

Local Insight, Global Ambition
